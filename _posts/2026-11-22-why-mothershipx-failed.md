title: Why MothershipX failed
date: 2026-11-22 12:00:00 +0400
description: What I’d do differently after three months of building and pivoting MothershipX.
---

We built [MothershipX]({{ '/mothershipx.dev/' | relative_url }}) to help people decide what to build. The first version was a curated list of startup ideas based on problems people talked about online. Some people paid for it. We changed direction before finding out what they’d paid for.

Over three months, we went from the idea browser to livestreaming and building together, then to a service that tried to build software autonomously. We eventually went back to helping people find ideas. The last version asked visitors to copy a prompt from our site and paste it into Claude or Codex. Few made that jump. We shut the project down.

If I were starting again, I’d talk to the people who paid for the first version before building the next one. I’d want to know what they’d hoped to find, what they actually used, and whether they’d come back for it.
