date: 2026-11-22 11:00:00 +0400
description: What joining an early team taught me about agreeing on my role before I start building.
---

I worked on the founding team at [Changewiser](https://changewiser.ai/), which makes software for managing organizational change. I was interested in the engineering and got started before we’d agreed on what my role would be. It took me months to see that we had different expectations, and I eventually stepped away.

Now I’d have that conversation before writing the first line of code. I’d ask what each of us is signing up to do, which decisions I’d own, and how we’d know whether the arrangement was working. Those questions would have been easier to answer before I got absorbed in building.
