Tornike Onoprishvili

I’m a cofounder of Singularity. We’re building AI teams that can launch a product, find its first customers, and improve it with their feedback. I previously cofounded Tegy, an AI tool for business strategy.

I started out simulating optical circuits, then spent several years building AI systems for startups. I built VIMAGE’s GPU backend and wrote the CUDA kernels for SimMS, which matched mass spectra about 1,000× faster than the MatchMS reference implementation in our benchmarks.

I sunset PeachJam when I realized a lower price wasn’t enough to make people switch app builders. MothershipX made early sales, but we pivoted before learning why people paid. I stepped away from Changewiser after realizing we hadn’t agreed on my role.
